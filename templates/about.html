About Smart Agro Vision

Smart Agro Vision is an advanced plant disease prediction system powered by Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN). Our mission is to help farmers, gardeners, and agricultural professionals quickly identify plant diseases and receive actionable treatment recommendations.

🎯 Project Goal

To provide an accessible, accurate, and fast plant disease detection system that leverages deep learning technology to support agricultural health and productivity.

How It Works

Our system uses a deep Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architecture trained to recognize various plant diseases from leaf images. Here's how the process works:

  1. Image Upload: Users upload a clear image of a plant leaf
  2. Preprocessing: The image is resized and normalized for model input
  3. CNN Analysis: The neural network analyzes the image through multiple layers
  4. Disease Classification: The model predicts the disease with confidence scores
  5. Results & Recommendations: Users receive disease information and treatment advice

CNN Architecture Details

Model Architecture

  • Input Layer: 224x224x3 RGB images
  • Convolutional Layers: 5 blocks with 32, 64, 128, 256, and 512 filters
  • Activation: ReLU activation functions
  • Normalization: Batch normalization after each conv layer
  • Pooling: Max pooling (2x2) for dimensionality reduction
  • Dense Layers: 512 and 256 neurons with dropout
  • Output Layer: Softmax activation for 38 disease classes
  • Optimizer: Adam optimizer
  • Loss Function: Categorical crossentropy

Supported Plants & Diseases

Our model can detect diseases in the following plants:

  • Apple (Scab, Black Rot, Cedar Apple Rust)
  • Tomato (Late Blight, Early Blight, Leaf Mold, Septoria, etc.)
  • Potato (Early Blight, Late Blight)
  • Corn/Maize (Common Rust, Gray Leaf Spot, Northern Leaf Blight)
  • Grape (Black Rot, Esca, Leaf Blight)
  • Cherry (Powdery Mildew)
  • Pepper (Bacterial Spot)
  • Peach (Bacterial Spot)
  • Strawberry (Leaf Scorch)
  • And many more...

Usage Instructions

  1. Navigate to the Home page
  2. Click "Choose Image" to select a plant leaf photo
  3. Ensure the image is clear and shows the leaf details
  4. Click "Predict Disease" to analyze
  5. Review the results and recommendations

⚠️ Important Notes

  • For best results, use clear, well-lit images of plant leaves
  • Ensure the leaf occupies most of the image frame
  • Avoid blurry or dark images
  • The model works best with leaves showing clear symptoms
